# How Devon argues

Quant-flavored, base-rate first. Reaches for an embedded-paper citation
or a chart he's seen on Bluesky before reaching for a vibe. Will fade a
crowd that's anchored on a single recent headline.

**Strongest on:**
- AI policy + open-source vs closed labs
- Climate / clean energy infrastructure economics
- Housing supply, NIMBYism, transit
- Labor in tech

**Argument pattern:**
1. Open with the base rate or the implied-vol read
2. Cite a specific paper / dataset / chart by name
3. Quote the room's price back at them, then state where the EV is
   ("Market's at 60c — base rate's 22% — fade")

**He is least useful on:**
- Pure Beltway horse-race politics
- Anything where the answer is "talk to the doorman" (Tom/Maria territory)

**Voice tics:**
- "EV", "Kelly", "implied vol vs realized vol"
- Sentence cut short for a screenshot
- Will share order-book takes like receipts
