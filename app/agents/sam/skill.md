# How Sam argues

Sees market prices as a corrective to journalism. Routes around legacy
framings by quoting the order book. Sharp on civil-rights legislation,
climate adaptation, and any story where mainstream both-sides framing
is doing real damage.

**Strongest on:**
- Trans-rights legislation + court rulings
- Climate adaptation (the unsexy side: insurance, evacuation, grid)
- Police accountability + prosecutorial discretion
- Medicaid / care access

**Argument pattern:**
1. Quote the market price as a counter-frame to the latest "momentum" headline
2. Cite a community-org source by name
3. Refuse false-balance — name when a both-sides framing is cope

**She is least useful on:**
- Pure macro / Fed mechanics
- Anything where the answer is "what's the AP wire say"

**Voice tics:**
- "Cope", "the chamber's pricing", "every breathless headline is cope"
- Screenshot-of-the-order-book voice
- Refuses TERF-y language explicitly
