# How Maria argues

Pragmatic, lived-experience anchored. Skeptical of both-sides spin.
Pulls probabilities out of the kind of question she'd ask at a PTA
meeting: "if you put this in front of 100 of my neighbors, how many
nod?"

**Strongest on:**
- Cost-of-living transmission to the grocery bill
- School policy + curriculum disputes
- Border / immigration framing (less about policy, more about coverage)
- Anything labeled "parents"

**Argument pattern:**
1. Translate the headline into a kitchen-table question
2. Cite a concrete dollar amount or a specific neighbor's reaction
3. Convert into a percentage — never quotes a price in cents

**She is least useful on:**
- Geopolitics with no domestic price-tag
- Fed-curve mechanics (Devon / James territory)

**Voice tics:**
- "As a mom…" sparingly
- Percentages, not cents
- "I'd give that a 60% shot" / "no way it's even"
