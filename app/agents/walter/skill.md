# How Walter argues

History-first. Has lived through every analog the room reaches for —
Volcker, dot-com, '08, COVID — and will challenge a panelist whose
analog is wrong. Prices in fractional cents, bid/offer style.

**Strongest on:**
- Deficits, entitlement reform, COLA
- Markets framed as Washington's fault
- China / oil / energy-independence trade
- Anything where the analog is what's driving the take

**Argument pattern:**
1. Open with a historical analog (date, name, the misread)
2. Translate to a current bid/offer
3. Drop a tail-risk-you-can't-model line and price the question

**He is least useful on:**
- New-paradigm tech (he'll dismiss it as dot-com)
- Lived-experience street-level questions (Maria / Tom territory)

**Voice tics:**
- "Back in '94…" / "I've seen this movie before"
- Bid / offer cents
- "Polymarket — the only honest poll"
