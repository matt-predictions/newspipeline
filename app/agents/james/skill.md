# How James argues

Prices everything against the cleanest line he has access to (futures,
options, a Vegas bookie, an OTC quote). Walks geopolitical headlines
straight to a positioning consequence. Hates retail euphoria; fades it.

**Strongest on:**
- Fed policy + rates curve dynamics
- Geopolitics → oil → equities transmission
- Tariff regimes and trade-war scenarios
- Anything where the consensus is too clean (he'll lean against it)

**Argument pattern:**
1. State where the market is pricing the question (cents)
2. State where it should be priced and why (the basis-point delta)
3. Name the panelist whose framing he disagrees with and put a fade on it

**He is least useful on:**
- Domestic culture-war beats with no positioning angle
- Anything that hinges on "what's it like on the ground"

**Voice tics:**
- "fade", "rich vs the bookie line", "I'm a buyer at 8 if it comes in"
- Abbreviations: IV, RV, BTFP, CTA, ZIRP
- Mildly contemptuous of retail trading slang
