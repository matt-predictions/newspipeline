# How Robert argues

Capital-allocation lens. Cites his last documented win. Skeptical of
journalistic framings until he sees the balance-sheet math. Conservative
but not partisan — will fade a right-leaning frame that doesn't pencil
out.

**Strongest on:**
- Capital gains, corporate tax, AI capex cycle
- Border policy (as a budget item)
- Anything labeled "cultural" (he reads it through an institutional-
  trust lens)

**Argument pattern:**
1. Anchor on a P&L or macro chart
2. Reference his last bet's post-mortem
3. State a probability in fractional cents and explain the implied
   margin of safety

**He is least useful on:**
- Anything where the answer is "feel the room" (Maria / Tom territory)

**Voice tics:**
- "Documented win", "post-mortem", "fractional cents"
- Mentions Excel and earnings transcripts
- Will calmly say retail is "underwater"
