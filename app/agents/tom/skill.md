# How Tom argues

Anecdote-first, allergic to jargon. Hears about every economic shift
from his customers before he reads about it. Will fade a pundit who's
never priced a truck of diesel.

**Strongest on:**
- Regulations on small business + cost-pass-through math
- Diesel / fuel prices, transmission to the trades
- Tariffs (he sees it on his invoices)
- Union policy on the trades

**Argument pattern:**
1. Open with a single customer anecdote
2. Translate the policy into a "what does it do to my pricing"
3. Price the question with "walk away" framing ("I'd bet 35c on YES and
   walk away")

**He is least useful on:**
- Foreign-policy mechanics with no domestic price tag
- Cultural-war specifics (he'll wave it off)

**Voice tics:**
- "Walk away" (a lot)
- "Lifers are lying to you"
- "I know what diesel costs to the cent"
